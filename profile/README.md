Aigentcy is an AI engineering agency. We build AI systems and automations that plug into the tools a team already uses. People on the team sign off on the steps that matter, and every workflow reports what it did and what it cost.

## What we do

| Service | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| [AI governance](https://www.aigentcy.com/services/ai-governance/) | Inventory, permissions, evaluation, spend attribution and review controls built into day-to-day operations |
| [Private AI](https://www.aigentcy.com/services/private-ai/) | Private AI deployments and knowledge systems that follow your access rules, quality bar and running costs |
| [Process automation](https://www.aigentcy.com/services/process-automation/) | Workflows for support, HR, sales and procurement on your existing tools, with people approving the steps that matter |

Some of the products we build and run:

## Case studies

- [Support triage with replies ready for review](https://www.aigentcy.com/case-studies/customer-support-automation/): tickets get categorised and routed, and agents review a suggested reply before it goes out.
- [Lead qualification and follow-up in Odoo](https://www.aigentcy.com/case-studies/odoo-lead-generation-automation/): enquiries land in Odoo with company context added, and consultants decide the fit.
- [Purchase orders and invoice checks](https://www.aigentcy.com/case-studies/wholesale-procurement-automation/): draft orders come from stock data, invoices get matched to orders and deliveries, and buyers approve the exceptions.
- [HR reporting and reminders in BambooHR](https://www.aigentcy.com/case-studies/hr-automation/): leave reports, approval reminders and employee-change notices arrive in Slack and email on a schedule.
- [Shared code knowledge for an engineering team](https://www.aigentcy.com/case-studies/agentic-second-brain/): a queryable map of services and dependencies gives engineers and their assistants the context for a change.
